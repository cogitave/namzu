Text in Namzu Desktop now follows one type scale. Replies, your messages and the message box are a little larger (15 px instead of 14 px). A reply's headings are calmer: the title is 20 px, the next heading 17 px and the rest read at the size of the text with a heavier weight, and bold in a reply is no heavier than those headings. A reply's table is 14 px with its numbers aligned in columns. The sidebar's rows, the result line, the row under the message box, buttons, menus and popups are 13 px, and small labels such as times, counts and badges are 12 px (nothing is drawn at 9.5, 10, 11 or 12.5 px any more). Times, durations, the added and removed counts, percentages and the cost beside the context percentage keep their width as they change. Nothing you could do is gone and no setting changed; fonts, colours and the terminal's font size are the same.
