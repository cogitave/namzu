The Pals, Projects and Recents headings in the sidebar now fold and unfold at any count, not only when there are more than three Pals. Folding hides the list without closing tabs or changing the open conversation, a small dot on a folded heading says when a Pal has a new message or a conversation is waiting, and the choice is remembered after a restart.
