Settings now opens as a dialog over the app instead of replacing it: the conversation, your draft and the panes stay where they were, and closing (the X, Esc or a click outside) returns you to the control you came from. The sections sit in four groups with the search at the top, and Settings reopens on the section you last visited, also after a restart. Nothing about the settings themselves changes.
