Settings now shows every Namzu config setting, in new Agent, Tools, Terminal and Telemetry sections and a "Namzu settings" group under General, Models, Projects and Updates. A change that widens what runs without asking asks first. Settings that hold commands or secrets, such as MCP servers, hooks and decision-model keys, show only whether they are set and stay in the config file. A conversation that is already open may keep the old value until it is started again.
