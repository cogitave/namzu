Switching from Ask first to Full access while Namzu is working now takes effect at once: the next call in that reply no longer asks, a card already on screen that Full access would not have shown is approved, and messages waiting in the queue follow the new mode. Cards that no mode settles (your own ask rules, protected paths, removing a home folder, the first look at the screen) stay open. A mode Namzu refuses is no longer left showing in the menu: it goes back to the previous mode and says why.
