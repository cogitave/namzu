Anonymous Zen users can now select all eight bundled free models instead of only Space Bunny. The seven previously blocked models use an experimental OpenCode-compatible request identity on the official Zen endpoint; Space Bunny keeps its verified direct request path, and keyed Zen and Zen Go requests are unchanged. Zen may still reject an experimental model or route, so a free catalogue entry is not a guarantee of service. The CLI labels this access as experimental and keeps Space Bunny as its default.
