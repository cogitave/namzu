The top of a conversation window is one 40 px bar instead of a 52 px strip of tabs. The bar shows the conversation's title and project, how full the conversation is as a pie with the share and cost ("14% · $0.06", no longer drawn beside the send button), and 28 px buttons for changes, terminal, project files, details and actions. The sidebar is now the one list of conversations: opening one from it replaces the conversation in front, and any other tabs you had open are closed the first time you do (a tab with an unsent draft stays, and Ctrl+Tab still reaches it). Conversations are never deleted by this. To get the strip back, choose View > Show conversation tabs, or Settings > Appearance > Show conversation tabs; the window is then as before. The conversation column is 720 px wide with 24 px gutters.
