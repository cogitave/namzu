A fourth decision point, `pick`: which control on the screen does an action name. Off unless named in `decision.points`, like the others. In `shadow`, the computer tool's `ui_act` accepts an `intent` and the decision model is asked, beside the action and without delaying it, which control the intent names; the decision log records how likely it thought the model's own choice was and whether its top choice agreed. In `on`, the tool also offers `ui_do`, which names a control in words and acts only when the decision model is sure and clearly ahead of the next control. The control names of the window leave the machine to the decision service; no screenshot or typed value does. The permission prompt for a `ui_do` reads "Press the control for "…"" since the control is chosen after approval.
