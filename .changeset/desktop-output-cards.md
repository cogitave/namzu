A reply that makes a page, a document, a picture, a sheet or slides now ends with a card for each: its name, what it is and how big, a line of its start, and a small picture of an image or page. Press a card to see the file beside the conversation; on hover or with the keyboard you can open it in the browser, open it with the app for that kind of file, copy its text, or save a copy where you choose. A file still being written says so, and one that could not be written says why. Files made by a command now show up as outputs too, in the cards and in the Outputs panel.
