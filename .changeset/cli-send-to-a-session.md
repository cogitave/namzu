You can now say something to a running session from another terminal: `namzu send <session> "<text>"` (or pipe the text in, or `--file`), and in `namzu watch` press `m`, type a line and press Enter. The session's own terminal takes it at its next request boundary (or starts a turn for it when idle) and the model is told it is your words from another terminal, not another agent and not an approval. It cannot answer an approval or a question, change the permission mode or run a command, and text starting with a slash is just text. The command says what happened and exits by it: delivered 0, accepted but not taken yet 10 (it stays queued in that terminal), refused 1, unreachable 69 (finished or gone), not found 66. Only a terminal of your account on this machine that has the conversation open reads messages; background and scheduled sessions do not. Nothing changes for existing commands, except that `namzu watch` in a terminal now shows "m to message it" where it showed "read-only" (a pipe or `--plain` is unchanged), and every terminal now records which conversation it has open in its live-session file.
