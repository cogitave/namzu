A small request made beside a turn (the history lookup at the start of a turn, the answer reviewer's inference, the eval judge's grade, the auto-review quick pass) no longer inherits the turn's thinking and effort. It asks for no reasoning where the driver can switch it off for one request, and where it cannot it carries 2,048 extra tokens of room for reasoning; a driver that refuses those controls before sending gets the request again without them. On a thinking model the history lookup used to spend its whole 512 tokens on reasoning and come back empty, and the next request then told the model that evidence was unavailable and it searched the disk instead of answering; an empty or cut-off lookup now adds nothing to the turn. `PreparationTextResult` gains an optional `finishReason`, and `chatStreamForSideRequest` and `sideRequestReasoning` are exported for hosts that make their own small requests. Delegated tasks created under the queue capacity behaviour are admitted before `createTask` returns, so the handle a caller gets back says `running` for a task that started instead of `pending`.
