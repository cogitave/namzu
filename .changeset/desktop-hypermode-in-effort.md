The effort panel has a *Hypermode* switch under the slider, for this conversation and any level, and the model button names it after the effort. `/effort hypermode on` and `/effort high hypermode on` do the same from the message box. The switch says why it cannot be turned on while *Dynamic workflows* is off in Settings. Settings no longer has a Hypermode row, and the workflow size rows are hidden while Dynamic workflows is off.
