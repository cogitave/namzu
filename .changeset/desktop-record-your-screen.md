Record your screen: on Windows, Customize, Skills, Add has a "Record your screen" item (only where the native helper is present and the computer is not a remote session). A chooser says what is recorded, then a small pill shows the time, with Pause, Done and Discard, while Namzu takes pictures of the display you chose, notes the controls you press, keeps text left in ordinary fields and, if you turn it on and listening is downloaded, your voice as text. Every Namzu window is hidden from the pictures, a password field pauses the recording, nothing is written to disk, and nothing goes anywhere until you read the recording on the review sheet and press Send. Namzu then asks the model to turn it into a skill through the usual save card. No key is ever recorded. Settings, Privacy has a switch to remove the menu item.
