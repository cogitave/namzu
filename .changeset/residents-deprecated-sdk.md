Residents are deprecated and will be removed in the next major release. Every resident export (`runResident`, `stepResident`, `ResidentHost`, `DiskResidentStore`, `DiskResidentAgenda`, `createResidentSelector`, `inspectResidentConsumption`, the outbox, history, tool-evidence and step-context helpers, and their types) is now marked `@deprecated` and behaves as before. Nothing is removed in this release. If you use them for long-running work, move to scheduled tasks (`LocalTaskScheduler`), session goals or background agents; learning (`runLearningCycle` and the other `Learning*` names) keeps working, but `runLearningCycle` still takes a resident agenda (`DiskResidentAgenda`) as its activation target and throws `ResidentConflictError` when it is paused, so a consumer of learning still needs the deprecated resident agenda store. Learning gets its own activation target before the residents are removed; that is the precondition for removing them in the next major release.
