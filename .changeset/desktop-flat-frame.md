The window looks flatter. The list of conversations and the conversation are two plain regions with one thin line between them: the sidebar no longer has rounded corners or a set-in look, and nothing in the frame casts a shadow. The strip of icons on the left now appears only while the sidebar is closed. While the sidebar is open, Settings (and the update button, when there is an update to install) sit in a row at the bottom of the sidebar, with the same names, tooltips and shortcut as before; the Settings and Plugins pages also keep a Home button there. Opening or closing the sidebar moves the conversation by the width that changes and no more, and the sidebar's header now ends on the same line as the top bar. Nothing you could do before is gone: each button is where it always was while the sidebar is closed, and in the sidebar's foot while it is open. Home from a conversation is the sidebar's New conversation row.
