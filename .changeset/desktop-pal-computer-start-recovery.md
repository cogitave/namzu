On Windows, pressing Start on a Pal's computer when its Linux image was already on the PC (after a reinstall, for example) now starts the computer instead of quietly going back to "Offline". A start that does not work out now says so under the row ("Kiro's computer could not start. Try again.") with a Details fold, a quiet "Try again" and one notice. Quitting no longer waits more than 5 seconds for the idle Linux machine to stop. Nothing to change on your side.
