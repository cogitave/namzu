Namzu Desktop now tells you from outside the window when a conversation is waiting for an approval or an answer: while no Namzu window is focused it shows a count on the app icon and flashes the taskbar or dock once, and clears both when you come back. There is no pop-up and no sound. To turn it off, use Settings > General > Show when a conversation needs you. Nothing to change on your side.
