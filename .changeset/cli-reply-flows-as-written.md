A reply now appears as it is written instead of a whole block at a time. In the terminal, a paragraph, a list item or a heading shows word by word as it arrives (redrawn at most about twelve times a second), a code fence shows its code a line at a time, and a table shows its header and every finished row. Nothing is drawn as something it is not and then replaced: a half-typed `**bold`, a link or backtick that has not closed, a row of dashes that may become a rule, a list marker with no text yet, a fence opener still being typed and a half-written table row all wait until they are decided. In the default screen each finished block is printed into the scrollback at once, together with your own message and everything before the reply, so the top of a long reply is never missing at a short window; a very long paragraph or code block is cut at a safe point so the live part stays small. The finished reply, a reopened conversation, `namzu watch` and an export look exactly as before, and fullscreen draws the same tail. A table, a long list item or a quote taller than the window is still clipped at its top until it is complete. A reply that a retry voids after part of it was printed stays above the new attempt.
