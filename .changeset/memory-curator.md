What a turn learns is no longer written as a record when the turn ends. The screened claims wait in a durable inbox under your Namzu home, and the small model curates them off the turn: for each it reads the related memories (titles and descriptions, never bodies) and adds a record, updates or replaces one a turn recorded, or does nothing because a memory, also one you or the assistant saved, already holds it. A replaced record is archived with links to its successor, never deleted; a memory you or the assistant wrote is never changed; a quiet `Remembered`, `Updated` or `Replaced` row reports what changed. Where no small model is available (the side route would be the conversation's own model) or it fails, the screened claims are written as before. Set `memory.curation: off` in your own config (a project file, or a profile in one, cannot set it) to restore the earlier behaviour exactly: no inbox, no model, the claims written when the turn ends. With it on, the small model sees the claims and the titles of related memories, on your own provider. The decision point `memory` now filters claims before the inbox.
