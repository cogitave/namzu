Menus and dialogs look the same everywhere. Every menu, popover and picker (the permission menu, the model picker, the plus menu, a conversation's and a project's actions, the engine panel and the effort panel) has rounder corners and less padding, rows that are 28 high (46 when a row has a description), one soft highlight for the pointer and the keyboard, a check at the right edge of the chosen row, and opens the same short distance from the control that opened it, which stays shaded while the menu is open. Dialogs are tighter: a confirmation is 400 wide with a smaller title, text and buttons, and the dimmed window behind it covers the sidebar and the header in both themes. Nothing you could do before is gone; the descriptions in the permission menu still wrap to a second line when they are long.

Settings is a calmer page. Its content is one column of at most 768 pixels in the middle of the window, with larger spaces between groups of settings and a smaller search field. Switches, chosen radio buttons and ticked boxes are now drawn in the text colour instead of green, so green stays for the one main action on a screen; an off switch is an outline with its knob on the left. The search overlay (Ctrl+K) is wider and roomier, sits a little below the middle of the window, shows a close mark at the right and a line of key hints at the bottom, and shows a row's shortcut or project only on the highlighted row. Nothing you could do before is gone.
