Adds `/auth` and `namzu --account <name>`. In a running conversation, `/auth` lists your accounts (provider, name, ready / limited until a time / needs sign-in, last used first, type to filter) and the one you pick is used from that conversation's next reply; other conversations keep theirs, and saved preferences are not written. `/auth add` adds a Codex account through the official `codex login` run with its own private folder, or a labelled API key with an optional address and starting model; `ctrl+r`, `ctrl+x` and `ctrl+a` in the list rename, remove, and let an API-key account switch automatically. When an account's allowance is spent the reply now stops with a plain sentence and one line, "enter continue with another account", which opens the list and runs the same message again on the account you choose; subscription accounts never switch on their own. `/login`, `/logout`, borrowed sign-ins and environment keys behave as before. A conversation that moves to another account records it, and a CLI older than this one will refuse to open such a conversation; one that stays on its usual sign-in is unaffected. `ProviderChoice` in `preferences.json` gains an optional `account`; files without it read as before.
