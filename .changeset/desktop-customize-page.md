Desktop has one Customize page for skills, connectors and plugins, in place of the Plugins page: a Customize button beside Extensions and Settings, a skill's detail with a switch, Try in chat and its files, a plugin's detail, and a table of tool servers with their approval. `/plugins`, `/mcp` and the saved-skill card's link open it on the matching tab. Nothing needs doing; the switch writes the same `skills.disabled` setting Settings does.
