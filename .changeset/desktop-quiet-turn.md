The conversation screen is quieter after a turn. The result line under a finished reply is now one line of text with no box: it takes a soft fill only when you point at it or tab to it, and its "..." menu shows on hover, on focus or when open (always on a touch screen). A failing check keeps its red dot and red words. The line over the work now says what was done ("Read 2 files, created a file, and 2 more · 36s") instead of "Worked for 36s, 7 steps", and has no rule under it until opened. Your own message has slightly tighter corners and can be a little wider, and inline code no longer has an outline. Nothing you could do is gone: the line still opens the changes, and Undo, Open file and Copy summary are still in the "...".
