New, additive: the first built-in learning generator. `proposeLearningCandidate` reads a domain's observations and earlier cycles, asks a model you supply (`LearningModel`, one function returning text and token counts) for one revision of an instruction skill, and returns the candidate with its typed edits, or refuses it. Every edit must sit on the editable surface (`resolveLearningSurface`, today the instruction skill only) and the wording must stay clear of the safety floor; a problem goes back to the model once and a second failure is refused, naming the part and never the text. `LEARNING_FLOOR_COMPONENTS` lists what is never editable and `resolveLearningSurface` throws if a configuration names one. `learningExperienceFromCycle` turns a finished cycle into an experience entry (adopted and helped, rejected and hurt or no observed effect). The proposal does not judge itself: it goes through `runLearningCycle` for paired verification, protection, confirmation and exact-revision activation. Nothing existing changes. New exports: `proposeLearningCandidate`, `resolveLearningSurface`, `learningExperienceFromCycle`, `LEARNING_SURFACE_KINDS`, `LEARNING_FLOOR_COMPONENTS` and the related types.
