Desktop has a details column: a button in the title bar shows where the conversation runs, its context, tool servers, the files it wrote and its changes with a button that opens them, as a 320 px column on a wide window and as a tab of the side panel on a narrower one. Pressing a file opens it beside the conversation. A document opens as a page with one slim header (its menu holds Open in app, Show in folder, Save a copy and the path), a floating outline button, lighter headings and square tables, and the context panel is lighter: a thinner bar, no section labels or notes on screen, and the sentence about shortening only once the conversation is 80% full.
