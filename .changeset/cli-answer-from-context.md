A follow-up such as "why did you mention X?" is answered from what the model said, instead of by opening files and searching the conversation and the disk. The system prompt, the context inventory and the history notes carry one rule (answer from what is visible; use the history tools only for what is no longer visible). The context inventory now appears only under window pressure or after a compaction and is worded as the model's own notes, not for the user. The history lookup logs why it did not run.
