"@namzu/cli": minor

`namzu spaces` now shows each space as tabs of tiled panes: a tab bar across the top of the workspace area, and under it a split tree of bordered panes, each pane its own Namzu session sized to its inside (the focused pane's border is light blue, the others dim grey; the borders fall back to the nearest 16 colours unless `COLORTERM` is `truecolor` or `24bit`). The keys are now one command after the prefix instead of a sticky navigate mode: `ctrl+b` then `v` or `-` splits, `h j k l` moves focus, `H J K L` swaps, `z` zooms, `r` enters resize mode, `x` closes the pane, `c` opens a tab, `n`/`p`/`1`-`9` switch tabs, `T` and `X` rename and close one, `N` `W` `D` create, rename and close a space, `w` moves between spaces, `b` hides the sidebar and `q` quits; `ctrl+b ctrl+b` still sends a literal `ctrl+b`. The earlier preview's keys (`n` new session, `j`/`k` between sessions, `r`/`R`/`d`/`D`, number keys for spaces) are gone. You can also click a pane, a tab or "+", drag a border to resize, and scroll the pane under the pointer. `spaces.json` is rewritten as version 2 with each tab's pane tree; an earlier file is read as one tab per session in the same order. Nothing changes for plain `namzu`.
