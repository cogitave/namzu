Settings gets switches the system applies, and nothing changes until you use them, except one: the system tray is on by default, so closing the last window now leaves Namzu running with an icon beside the clock (choose Quit from it, or turn *System tray* off under Desktop app to quit on close as before). New: *Run on startup* (Windows and macOS), *Keep computer awake*, *Keep computer awake while working* with *Keep awake on battery power* (under Agent), and *Pause downloads on low data* (Windows; app and engine downloads that start by themselves wait while the connection is metered). A change the system refuses is said in one line under the row. Old settings files load with the defaults.
