A conversation you continue with `namzu -c` or `/resume` shows a block you pasted as its chip (`[Pasted text #1 +40 lines]`), the way it looked when you sent it, instead of the whole text. `/rewind` lists the message by the chip, and choosing it puts the full pasted text back in the message box. The title of a conversation that starts with a paste, and the `Resumed:` line, show the chip too. Conversations saved before this show the text as before.
