Namzu Desktop gains voice input in Turkish: a microphone button in the composer, an in-app agreement the first time you use it, and a Voice input card in Settings, Speech where the one-time download (about 1 GB) lives. What you say is turned into text on this computer and sent as a normal message; saying "dur" stops a running reply, a question waiting on screen is never answered by voice, and a reply you started by voice is read aloud when the Turkish voice is installed. Desktop now answers microphone permission requests itself: only its own windows, audio only, after you agree; every other permission is unchanged. A spoken message keeps a conversation on Auto, as a typed one does. Nothing to do on upgrade: voice input stays off until you download it.
