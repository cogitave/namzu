The decision points have one-word names: `notify`, `memory` and `skill` in `decision.points`. The names they were released under, `notify-worthy`, `memory-admit` and `skill-worthy`, still work and mean the same points, so a config you already wrote keeps working; name a point both ways and it is refused. Move to the new names when you next edit the file.
