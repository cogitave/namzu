Desktop now shows a system notification while no Namzu window is focused when a conversation needs your answer, a reply stops with an error, or a reply that took 30 seconds or more finishes, however it ended short of an error. It shows the project's name and a fixed sentence, never conversation text, and clicking it opens the conversation. Settings > General has a switch for each of the three and one for a sound, which is off. Nothing to do on upgrade; turn the switches off to keep only the icon count.
