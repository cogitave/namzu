The sidebar, tab strip, terminals and command palette are easier to read and drive. There is one **Add new project** button, on the Projects heading; a project row no longer has a one-click ×, and its **…** menu holds Open folder, Archived conversations and Remove project…; each conversation is listed once (Recents shows only what a project's list is not showing, with the project's name); an **Archived conversations** entry in the sidebar lists every project; Archive has no ellipsis and its Undo stays 20 seconds. Two terminals of one project are named `sh · app` and `sh 2 · app`, and a terminal's dot says what it means on hover. The front tab always scrolls into view and a **Show all tabs** menu lists the rest; a caret beside **+** and the terminal tab menu open the split entries, which close the docked sidebar to make room instead of scrolling the page sideways. New keys: Ctrl+W (⌘+W) closes a tab, Ctrl+Tab, Ctrl+Shift+Tab and Ctrl+1 to 9 switch, Ctrl+Shift+PageUp and PageDown reorder, Ctrl+Shift+backslash opens a terminal to the right, and Ctrl+, works from a terminal (inside a terminal Ctrl+W still belongs to the shell; Ctrl+F4 closes the tab). Terminal find and the multi-line paste confirmation are announced in the pane. Search ignores Turkish I, ı, İ and i differences and no longer breaks under a Turkish system language. The palette says "conversations", lists recent ones, Archived, tab and split actions, and shows a chord as one keycap. A conversation reopens at the scroll position you left it, and a finished Codex, Claude Code or Namzu update closes its own terminal tab.
