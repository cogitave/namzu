When Namzu needs an answer from you, it now asks in a card above the message box. Before, a question from the agent had nowhere to appear: it could only guess, or run a command that waits for a keyboard nobody has. The card shows one question at a time, each choice with a number you can press and a line saying what it means, a "Recommended" mark on the choice Namzu suggests, an "Other" row to answer in your own words, and boxes you can tick when you may pick several. Skip (Esc) carries on without an answer; Send (Ctrl+Enter) hands your answers over. Enter on the main keys and on the number pad both work. The conversation shows "Question waiting" while it asks, stopping the reply closes the card, and the Questions row in Settings > Notifications now does something.
