The window frame and the sidebar line up on one set of sizes. The bar above a conversation and the sidebar's header are both 44 px high, with the strip of tabs or without it and in Settings, so switching between a conversation and Settings moves nothing, and the line under the wordmark meets the line under the tabs. Every row in the sidebar is 32 px high and its text starts at the same place, whether it is New conversation, a Pal, a project, a conversation or a Settings section; a conversation's state mark (running, unread, error, waiting for you) now sits at the left of its row instead of the right, and conversations are no longer pushed in under their project. Section titles are lighter, the foot of the sidebar is a 48 px strip with a line above it, and the selected row is a little brighter.
