The window frame and the sidebar line up on one set of sizes. The bar above a conversation and the sidebar's header are both 36 px high, with the strip of tabs or without it, in Settings and above the collapsed rail, so switching between a conversation and Settings moves nothing. Only the main side draws the line under its header: the sidebar's header has none, the home screen has none, and Settings, Plugins and Pals now get the same line across the pane that a conversation has. Rows in the sidebar are 32 px high with 2 px between them and rounder corners, and every label starts at the same place whether it is New conversation, a Pal, a project, a conversation or a Settings section; a conversation's state mark (running, unread, error, waiting for you) sits at the left of its row instead of the right, and conversations are no longer pushed in under their project. Section titles sit a little further from the rows above them, are a touch firmer, and start in line with the icons; the buttons at the end of a row are smaller. The foot of the sidebar is a 45 px strip with a line above it and smaller buttons, and the selected row is a little brighter in both themes.
