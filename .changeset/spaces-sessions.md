`namzu spaces` now lists your project folders as spaces, each with one dot for its most urgent session (red needs approval, blue finished and not yet seen, yellow working, green idle), and the selected space's sessions below. Press `ctrl+b` then `n` for a new session, `N` for a new space from a folder, `r`/`R` to rename, `d`/`D` to close, `J`/`K` or `1`-`9` to change space; click rows and scroll with the mouse. Options after `spaces` now go to the first session only, and the number keys pick a space instead of a session. Status comes from each session's own live record, with no screen reading. Plain `namzu` is unchanged.
