A project whose folder is gone now appears in the composer's project chooser marked "Folder not found" and cannot be chosen. A message you send to a Pal also shows in that Pal's own conversation, quoted, with where it stands ("Waiting for the Pal to read it"), and the Pal's row in the sidebar shows a dot until you open it; nothing here starts the Pal. Delete Pal has an Open folder button that shows the Pal's folder. The Stop button's tooltip says why Queue is off. The File menu has Close tab, Next tab and Previous tab, and Ctrl+W now closes a terminal tab as well (Ctrl+C, Ctrl+D and the other keys stay the program's). Message times read "Sent at ..." for your messages and "Received at ..." for replies everywhere.
