Computer use screenshots are sized by the model answering the turn: an Anthropic model of version 4.7 or later gets the high-resolution tier (up to 2576 px), every other model the standard 1568 px tier, and the choice follows Auto routing and `/model`. The model is also no longer told to zoom on a shrunken display screenshot. Nothing to change on your side; a high-resolution display now reads small text without a zoom step.
