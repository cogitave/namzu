The message box is one line when it is empty, with slightly tighter corners, and the control at its right is Send only once you have typed something or attached a file (the voice control while the box is empty, Stop while a reply runs). The box now sits at the bottom of the window on the home screen as well, exactly where it is in an open conversation, so it no longer jumps down when you send your first message; the heading and the three starters are centred in the space above it, and the heading stays put when the box grows with an attachment or a longer draft until the box would reach it, when the starters go first. The row of settings under the box is a little shorter, sits closer to the window's bottom edge and no longer keeps a gap at its right end for an arrow that only showed under the pointer. The box, that row and the conversation's text are all one column that keeps at least 32 px free on each side of the pane (16 px in a narrow pane).
