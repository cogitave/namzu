A turn now has a record of what it changed and what checked it, built from its own tool calls and never from the model's answer: `Turn.outcome` and `BaseAgentResult.outcome` (type `TurnOutcome`, verdicts `unchanged`, `checked`, `stale`, `failing`, `inconclusive`, `unchecked`). `checked` means a recognised check passed and the workspace did not change after it; it does not mean the check covers the change, and a check run through a pipe, `;`, `||` or `&` is `masked` and never counts as passed. The record is on `turn_completed` and `turn_failed` as an optional `settlement.outcome`, on `child_session_ended.outcome`, on the workflow journal's `agent_outcome` and, optionally, in the checkpoint; a turn resumed from a checkpoint without it reports no outcome. `TurnConfig.outcome` takes `detectChanges` (a workspace fingerprint, `fingerprintWorkspace` is one), `isCheck` and `detectTimeoutMs` so shell-made changes are seen. The `Agent` tool's `data.outcome` carries a child's record and one kernel-written line follows the child's marked block (`describeOutcomeRecord`, `KERNEL_RECORD_PREFIX`); `untrustedEnvelopeBody` skips one trailing line of that kind. `ToolCallOutcome.facts` is the typed result of a call. Nothing changes for a host that ignores the new fields. A reader built before this release refuses a session log settlement that carries `outcome`, like any field it does not know; keep readers current before a log is shared with them.
