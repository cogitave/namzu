A turn now has a record of what it changed and what checked it, built from its own tool calls and never from the model's answer: `Turn.outcome` and `BaseAgentResult.outcome` (type `TurnOutcome`, verdicts `unchanged`, `checked`, `stale`, `failing`, `inconclusive`, `unchecked`, `unknown`). `checked` means a recognised check passed and the workspace did not change after it; it does not mean the check covers the change, and a check run through a pipe, `;`, `||` or `&` is `masked` and never counts as passed. The record is on `turn_completed` and `turn_failed` as an optional `settlement.outcome`, on `child_session_ended.outcome`, on the workflow journal's `agent_outcome` and, optionally, in the checkpoint; a turn resumed from a checkpoint without it reports no outcome. `TurnConfig.outcome` takes `detectChanges` (a workspace fingerprint, `fingerprintWorkspace` is one), `isCheck` and `detectTimeoutMs` so shell-made changes are seen. The `Agent` tool's `data.outcome` carries a child's record and one kernel-written line follows the child's marked block (`describeOutcomeRecord`, `KERNEL_RECORD_PREFIX`); `untrustedEnvelopeBody` skips one trailing line of that kind. `ToolCallOutcome.facts` is the typed result of a call. The record is always written, so a session log written by this release cannot be read by an earlier SDK that validates a settlement strictly: it refuses any `turn_completed`, `turn_failed` or `child_session_ended` that carries `outcome`. Treat that as a downgrade break and update every reader of a shared log before this release writes to it. A host that only reads `Turn` and `BaseAgentResult` is otherwise unaffected.
