New, optional: a model can hand a task back to a person. Set `TurnConfig.handBack: true` and the turn offers `hand_back_task` (a `kind` of `conflict`, `missing` or `decision`, what blocks it, what it tried, what a person must decide or provide) and adds one short paragraph to the static system prompt (98 tokens by the kernel's estimate, 186 more for the tool). An accepted call ends the turn with no further model call, and the other calls in that step are refused instead of run. The fact is `Turn.handedBack` and `LiveTurnSettlement.handedBack`, in memory; the session log and the persisted outcome record are not changed, since an earlier release validates them strictly, and a reader recovers it from the saved `hand_back_task` call and its receipt (`HAND_BACK_RECEIPT`). New exports: `HAND_BACK_TOOL_NAME`, `HAND_BACK_RECEIPT`, `HAND_BACK_GUIDANCE`, `buildHandBackTool`, `parseHandBack`, `describeHandBack`, `handBackModelLines`, `handBackReason`, `handedBackScorer`, `EvalTurn.handedBack`, the types `HandBack` and `HandBackKind`, `GoalConfirmation.handBack` and `GoalEndingReport.handedBack` (a goal round handed back ends not confirmed and blocked). Nothing changes unless you set it: with `handBack` absent or false the tool is not registered and the prompt is byte for byte as before. A tool of your own named `hand_back_task` is only affected when `handBack` is true.
