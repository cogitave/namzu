The learning cycle now refuses to learn from, or produce, anything that carries evaluator-side material. `runLearningCycle` checks the active skill baseline, the failure evidence (before any stage or journal entry), exploration observations and the generated candidate against the built-in grader-verdict and answer-key wording and secret shapes, plus an optional `leakGuard` you supply (path globs, planted canaries from `createLearningCanary()`, extra markers, verbatim protection-task text, literal secrets). A hit ends the cycle with status `failed`, a reason that says it is a leak and not a false positive, and `result.leak` listing the kind, subject and rule; the matched text is never repeated and a refused candidate is not returned. New exports: `createLearningCanary`, `scanForLearningLeak`, `assertNoLearningLeak`, `LearningLeakError` and the types `LearningLeakGuard`, `LearningLeakFinding`, `LearningLeakKind`, `LearningLeakSubject`. What to check: evidence that legitimately mentions a verdict, an answer key or a secret-shaped string is now refused. Remove or redact it at the source; there is no switch to turn the built-in checks off. Canaries are also found inside larger base64 or base64url blobs at any alignment and in uppercase hex; the built-in secret shapes are found through zero-width characters. The CLI passes no `leakGuard`, so it applies only the built-in checks.
