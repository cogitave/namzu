`/resume` and `namzu resume` can now find a conversation: type to narrow the list by name, first message or last message, press ctrl+a to include the conversations of every folder (those open from their own folder), and press ctrl+r to rename the selected one in place. The preview shows the first lines of the opening message. Typed letters in the picker used to be ignored and now search; nothing needs changing.
