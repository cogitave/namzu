Archived conversations now live in the sidebar's Recents section instead of a row at the bottom and a dialog. A quiet archive button at the right of the Recents heading switches the list, in place, to the archived conversations of every project and back; each archived row has a Restore icon. The command palette's "Archived conversations" and the conversation details menu switch the same view, and the toast after archiving says where to find the conversation.
