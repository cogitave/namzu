A reply no longer ends with a list of cards for every file it wrote. Only a document made by the document skill gets a card, now one compact row with its name and a state word; code, pages, pictures and plain files show in the activity line, in the reply's links and in the Outputs list. The activity line shows the lines added and keeps a short reply's time in its tooltip, and the line at the end of a reply is quieter.
