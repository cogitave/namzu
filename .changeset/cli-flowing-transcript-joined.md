The three terminal changes that arrived together (a reply drawn as it is written, painting only what changed, and the quieter rows and working region) now also hold when they meet, and the default screen keeps one promise: it never erases what the terminal already holds. Text printed before you started Namzu and the conversation rows already printed stay, once each and in order, through a resize, `/compact` and `/clear`. `/compact` no longer clears the screen and writes the conversation again: the rows above stay as the record and one row says the messages above are summarized for the model, with the conversation going on under it. `/clear`, `/new` and `/resume` clear the visible screen only (the rows on it move up into the scrollback and the scrollback is never erased); the last few rows still in the live region when you clear are not kept. A window made shorter while a long reply is being written no longer prints a row of it twice. A reply you cut off with Esc in the middle of a table ends the table at its last whole row and shows what was written of the unfinished row as a line of plain text, not as a row of empty cells. The part of a reply still being written is kept short enough that it, the plan under the working line, the running calls and a queued message fit the window together. `NO_COLOR=1` on its own now turns colour off in the whole terminal UI; before it only switched off the tint behind your message. Two things you may notice: a reopened conversation has no plan checklist block and its closing rows can sit in a different order than they did live, and after a narrowing resize the rows already printed are wrapped by the terminal and can break in the middle of a word. Nothing to do on your side.
