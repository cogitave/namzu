With teammates on, more of the team's transcript is drawn as bordered boxes: a task handed to a teammate, a request to stop it, its refusal, a plan waiting for you and your answer to it, and, in a teammate's own transcript, a warning box while its call or plan waits on you. Esc in an open teammate that is working now cuts its current turn short instead of leaving the view: it stays on the team, goes idle and can be messaged again (the key line says `esc to interrupt`; for an idle one it says `esc to return to team lead` and Esc goes back). The folded `N idle agents` row on the rail can be selected and opened with Enter, and a teammate row's key line names `ctrl+x ctrl+k to stop all agents` when more than one agent runs on a line at least 90 columns wide. A teammate's expanded idle row shows `Last message: <summary>` when it wrote to the lead that turn. Wording changes: the refusal, plan request and plan answer rows are boxes now (the answer keeps its words, `Plan from @name approved` or `Plan from @name rejected`); anything that read those rows from the screen as single lines needs to read the box. Teams-off transcripts do not change.
